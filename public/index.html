How to play

One person is the spy. Everyone else shares a secret location and gets a unique role there.

Take turns asking each other questions about the location. Non-spies want to flush out the spy without giving the location away. The spy tries to blend in and figure out where you are.

When the timer ends, vote out loud on who you think the spy is — then the host taps End round & reveal to see who was right.

Setup

The host creates a room and shares the 4-letter code. Other players open the same site and tap Join a room.

Leave the game?

You're in the middle of a round. Your spot is held for 30 seconds if you want to rejoin.
